About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

History and Institutional Background

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. It began in 1979 as Universiti Pertanian Malaysia's Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science in Mengabang Telipot, providing facilities for students and lecturers to conduct practical sessions and research in marine science and fisheries.

Following its transformation into Kolej Universiti Terengganu (KUT) in 1999 and subsequently Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM) in 2001, the institution was officially granted full university status on 1 February 2007 and renamed Universiti Malaysia Terengganu.

Today, UMT is recognized as Malaysia's premier university focusing on marine science and aquatic resources, offering a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs across oceanography, fisheries, computer science, maritime management, and environmental studies.
