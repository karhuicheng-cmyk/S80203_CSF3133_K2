About the Event

Keynote Speaker: Dr. Ali

Dr. Ali is a leading AI researcher with over 15 years of industry experience. She will be speaking about the future of automated code generation and how students can adapt to modern development tools.

Activity Feature: Core Hackathon Challenge

Put your skills to the test in our 3-hour rapid prototyping challenge. Students will work in groups to solve real-world community problems using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with exciting prizes for the top teams.
